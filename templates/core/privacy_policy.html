Applies To: All students, parents, exhibitors, and attendees.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for the KNOVA Education Expo, download digital passes, or enquire with institutions, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and city of residence.
  • Academic preferences, intended study destinations, fields of interest, and budget range.
  • Badge scanning history and interactions with university booths during the expo.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide a tailored, world-class education fair experience:

  • To issue your Fast-Track QR Badge and manage event check-ins.
  • To connect you with universities and scholarship programs that match your academic profile.
  • To send essential updates regarding seminars, schedule changes, and venue logistics.
  • To facilitate post-event counseling and direct admissions inquiries upon your consent.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell your personal data to third-party brokers. Your contact details are only shared with participating universities whose booths you voluntarily visit and allow your badge to be scanned by.

We implement industry-standard encryption, strict database access controls, and secure data handling adhering to GCC and international privacy standards.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account and badge records at any time by contacting our support team at privacy@knova.gcc.
