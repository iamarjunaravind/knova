Applies To: All registered visitors, university delegates, and exhibitors.

1. Admission & Registration

Admission to the KNOVA Global Education Expo is free for students, parents, and academic counselors. All visitors must obtain a verified digital ticket or QR badge online prior to entering the venue halls.

Badges are non-transferable and must be presented at security checkpoints and entrance scanners upon request.

2. Event Code of Conduct

KNOVA is committed to providing a professional, respectful, and safe environment for all attendees and participating universities:

  • Respectful interactions with institutional delegates, university faculty, speakers, and fellow attendees are required at all times.
  • Disruptive behavior, harassment, unauthorized solicitation, or commercial canvassing outside designated exhibitor booths is strictly prohibited.
  • Attendees must adhere to local GCC venue safety regulations and emergency procedures.

3. Scholarship & University Offers

Information presented by participating universities regarding tuition fees, scholarships, visa sponsorship, and admissions eligibility is subject to the individual policies and final review of each respective institution. KNOVA provides a connection platform and does not independently guarantee academic admissions.

4. Media & Photography

By entering the KNOVA event premises, attendees grant permission for official photography and videography to be used for educational recap reports, promotional highlights, and archival purposes.
